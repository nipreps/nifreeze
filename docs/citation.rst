============================================

Writing a paper about NiFreeze
------------------------------

NiFreeze follows the NiPreps community's guidelines on
`recognizing contributions and publications
<https://www.nipreps.org/community/CONTRIBUTING/#recognizing-contributions>`__:

- Anyone listed as a maintainer or a contributor may start a manuscript about NiFreeze,
  as its first author.
- Every maintainer is included as an author, unless they opt out,
  and every contributor is invited to be one.
- First authorship goes to those who started the manuscript, carried it through and wrote it:
  start from the default author list below, and move your name to the front.
- A preprint is posted (e.g., on `arXiv <https://arxiv.org>`__ or
  `bioRxiv <https://www.biorxiv.org>`__) before publication.

The maintainers, contributors and principal investigators are listed in the
`.maint/ <https://github.com/nipreps/nifreeze/tree/main/.maint>`__ folder of the repository.
The contributors to *eddymotion*, whose code NiFreeze continues, are credited in
`.maint/EDDYMOTION.md <https://github.com/nipreps/nifreeze/blob/main/.maint/EDDYMOTION.md>`__.
