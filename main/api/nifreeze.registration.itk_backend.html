    nifreeze.registration.itk_backend module¶

    Registration with ITK’s registration framework (the …v4 classes of ITK 5).

    ITK’s framework optimizes several transforms together, which elastix’s does not. So this backend can estimate each volume’s mapping as head motion followed by a field along the phase-encoding direction, \(W = E M\), with 9 parameters (6 rigid, and the phase-encoding row of \(E\); see split_phase_encoding()), when the settings say restrict: phase-encoding. Otherwise it estimates a full affine (12 parameters). With motion: fixed as well, the head motion stays as the start gives it (in the eddy-current pass, the head-motion step’s estimate), and only the field’s 3 parameters are estimated, about the grid’s centre. The estimates are then not split: the eddy-current pass stores them as it stores elastix’s, with the head motion it holds factored out and kept unchanged.

    The images reach ITK in their voxel frame (identity direction, origin zero, the voxel spacing), so ITK’s physical axes are the voxel axes, and the restriction to the phase-encoding axis, an axis of the data array, is exact on oblique data. The estimate goes back to RAS+. The images are prepared as the elastix backend prepares them: clipped to the sample’s window and coded to 12 bits (as clip selects), then winsorized and histogram-matched when the settings ask. Only the fixed image is masked.

    Settings are the itk sections of AFFINE (or of any such file), with one registration per stage. A stage samples its metric on a share of the grid (sampling_percentage), or at a number of points within the mask (samples), as elastix’s random samplers do: its cost then does not grow with the grid.
